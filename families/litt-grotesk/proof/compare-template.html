Kanton Grotesk · v0.1 → v0.2

The same screen, before and after the UI pass.

Both windows use identical markup and CSS. Only the font file changes. Pins mark each fix, explained below.

Beforev0.1 · 95 characters
Afterv0.2 · 370 characters
1

Labels sit in the middle

v0.1 centred its line box on the lowercase, so capitals rode high in buttons and tags. v0.2 centres it on the capitals. The close-up shows the shift; centre lines show it at real size.

2

No fallback letters

ü, Ł, ę, Ø, ţ, €, curly quotes, dashes, → and … came from another font in v0.1, in a different weight and shape. v0.2 draws all 370 itself.

3

Codes stay readable

In v0.1, I, l and 1, and O and 0, were near-identical. With ss01 on the input, v0.2 adds a tailed l, a serifed I and a slashed zero.

4

Caps punctuation lines up

With case on, brackets and the middle dot rise to cap height in uppercase labels. In v0.1 they hang toward the baseline.
