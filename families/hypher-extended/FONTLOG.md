# FONTLOG for Hypher Extended

## Sources

Drawn in code; no outlines are taken from another font. Proportions, stroke weights and corners are measured from, and fitted to, a reference wordmark image (`proof/reference.png`).

## Changelog

- **0.100 (2026-11-18):** first prototype. A–Z (lowercase maps to the capitals), figures, punctuation, `& %`, at 300–700, with a starter kerning list.
